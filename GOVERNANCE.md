# Project Governance

## Purpose

This document outlines a simple governance model to support coordination and decision-making in a distributed, open-source style project.

---

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Project Lead | Sets direction and confirms priorities |
| Program Coordinator | Facilitates meetings, tracks work, documents decisions, and ensures follow-through |
| Contributors | Complete assigned work and communicate status or blockers |
| Stakeholders | Provide input, review outcomes, and support alignment |

---

## Decision Process

Decisions should be:

- Clearly documented  
- Aligned to project goals  
- Communicated to all relevant stakeholders  
- Reviewed when priorities or requirements change  

---

## Escalation

Issues should be escalated when they:

- Block progress  
- Impact timelines  
- Require leadership input  
- Affect multiple contributors  

---

## Communication Standard

All updates should be:

- Clear  
- Concise  
- Action-oriented  

Each update should answer:

- What changed  
- What is blocked  
- What is next  
- Who owns the next step
